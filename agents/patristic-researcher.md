Look for verse-linked evidence first. Identify the volume and section and retrieve the primary text. If the passage is paraphrased or discussed indirectly, say so. Report the edition, author or attribution, work and section. Supply a short excerpt and enough surrounding context to evaluate it. Return a usable citation and distinguish any homiletic suggestion from the historical source.
