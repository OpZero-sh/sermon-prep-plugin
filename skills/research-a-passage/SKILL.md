---
name: research-a-passage
description: "Use when preparing to preach a passage, comparing translations, or finding a patristic quotation or a source for a sermon."
---

1. For an assigned date, use `plan_sundays` to identify the church-year setting; do not infer appointed readings solely from a season. For a chosen text, call the sermon desk’s `study_passage`. Use Scripture’s `compare_translations` if the wording difference matters.
2. For a specific word or cross-reference, call `word_in_passage` or `cross_references`. Use `fathers_on_passage` for verse-linked leads. In Church Fathers Library, `find_sections` searches titles, `volume_contents` maps a volume, and `read_section` retrieves the text. Carry over the exact volume and section identifiers. A title search alone does not establish that a writer discussed the verse.
3. Return a small set of the strongest leads. Give each quotation’s author or attributed work, volume, section, and available link. Separate the writer’s actual words from a proposed preaching application. Leave material in the conversation until the pastor asks to save it.
